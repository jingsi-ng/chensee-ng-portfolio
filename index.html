The Archivist

Why an archive

I came to digital media the long way round, through Digital Humanities and four years of teaching. That is why I care less about making things look new, and more about making them legible: a visitor should know where they are and what they are looking at within seconds.

So I treat every site as a record worth keeping. Catalogued, labelled, built to outlast a trend cycle, and opened one file at a time.
