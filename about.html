Overview

The long way round

I didn’t start in digital media. I studied Digital Humanities at Dundalk IT, then spent four years teaching secondary school in Malaysia before coming back to Ireland for my master’s.

Teaching turned out to be the most useful design training I could have had. Standing in front of a class every day is a brutal usability test: if the way you’ve structured something is confusing, thirty people will tell you immediately. That’s where the archive idea behind this site comes from: I care about things being findable and clearly labelled, not just attractive.

These days I spend most of my time in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, moving into Astro and Supabase for anything that needs accounts and a database, and p5.js when a piece needs to be drawn rather than laid out. I like the part of the job where a design decision has to survive contact with real content.
